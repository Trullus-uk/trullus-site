# Privacy Policy **Trullus** Last updated: 6 June 2026 --- ## Who we are Trullus is a mobile app that lets you save and share personal recommendations with friends, from your neighbourhood to your next holiday destination. Trullus is operated by Watermelon Life Limited, 124-128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: hello@trullus.uk --- ## What information we collect **Information you give us directly** When you create an account, we collect your first name, last name, and email address. You also choose a username (handle) which is shown to other users. When you use the app, we collect the recommendations, locations, and personal notes you choose to add. This is the content you deliberately create and share. **Information from third-party services** When you search for a place or destination, we use the Google Places API to retrieve location information including place names, addresses, photos, and coordinates. We store some of this data (place name, address, category, coordinates, and a photo reference) as part of your recommendations. **Information collected automatically** We collect basic information about how you use the app through our backend provider, Supabase, including when you sign in and when data is created or updated. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking tools. --- ## How we use your information We use the information we collect to: - Create and manage your account - Show your recommendations to your friends on the app - Allow friends to find you by name or username - Show you your friends' recommendations and activity - Generate shareable links to your recommendations - Provide customer support if you contact us We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes. --- ## Who we share your information with **Other Trullus users** Your name, username, and the recommendations you add are visible to users you are connected with as friends on the app. If you share a link to your recommendations, the recipient can see your name, username, and the recommendations for that location, regardless of whether they have a Trullus account. **Service providers** We use the following third-party services to operate the app: - **Supabase** — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account data and recommendations are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase is GDPR compliant. See their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy. - **Google Places API** — used to search for and retrieve place information when you add a recommendation. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. - **Expo / EAS** — used to build and distribute the app. See their privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy. We do not share your data with any other third parties. --- ## Location data The app requests access to your device location to show you relevant nearby recommendations from your network. Location access is optional — you can use the app without granting location permission. We do not store your device's real-time location. Coordinates stored in the app relate to places you have explicitly added as recommendations or locations, not your live position. --- ## Your rights You have the right to: - **Access** the personal data we hold about you - **Correct** inaccurate data — you can edit your name and handle in the app settings - **Delete** your account and all associated data — available in the app under Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, locations, and recommendations. - **Object** to how we use your data - **Data portability** — request a copy of your data in a portable format To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@trullus.uk. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. --- ## Data retention We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. Some anonymised, aggregated data (such as total recommendation counts) may be retained for analytical purposes but cannot be linked back to you. --- ## Children Trullus is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. --- ## Security We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure authentication through Supabase, and row-level security policies that ensure users can only access data they are permitted to see. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you have concerns about the security of your data, please contact us. --- ## Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The date at the top of this policy shows when it was last updated. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy. --- ## Contact For any questions or requests relating to this privacy policy: **Email:** hello@trullus.uk **Address:** London, UK --- *This privacy policy covers the Trullus mobile application available on Android and iOS.*
